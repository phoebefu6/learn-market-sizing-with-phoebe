# Learn Market Sizing with Phoebe

Market sizing as evidence for a decision, in ten 45-minute sessions on two tracks.

- **Business (b1-b4, no code):** what a market size is for, reading a sizing deck, who owns which
  half (the evidence and the bet), and getting from a size to a plan.
- **Analyst (a1-a6, hands-on in the browser):** top-down, bottom-up, analogy and proxy, ranges and
  the tornado, share realism and the collision test, and a triangulation bench.

Every widget computes in the browser on real rows from the Statistics Bureau of Japan (2020
Population Census households; Family Income and Expenditure Survey tea spend per household,
2023-2025 average). The running company, Kettle & Co, and all of its inputs are constructed.

Static site: open `index.html`, or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server`.

By Phoebe Fu.
