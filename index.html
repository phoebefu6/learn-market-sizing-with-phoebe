Learn Market Sizing with Phoebe

"How big is the market?" is usually asked as if it had one answer. It has as many answers as there are categories, populations and assumptions, and the useful one is the answer to a decision somebody has to make. These ten sessions split the work the way it splits in real teams. Four Business sessions are for the people who ask for a size and place the bet: what a size is for, how to read a sizing deck, who owns which half, and how to get from a size to a plan you can defend. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who build the evidence: top-down from public statistics, bottom-up from your own data, analogy and proxy, ranges, share realism, and a triangulation bench that runs on Japan's real household survey in your browser. The running company, Kettle & Co, is constructed.
